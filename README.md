---

I'm Sandeep, a software architect in Bangalore. 17 years of building systems: subscription
engines at Intuit, network management at Juniper, retail price prediction at BlueYonder, Web3
infrastructure, and now healthcare at [GlucoAI](https://myglucoai.com). I like software that solves
one problem well, runs on cheap hardware, and stays useful long after the hype moves on.

- 🏗️ Day job: Co-founder & CTO at [GlucoAI](https://myglucoai.com), architecting a mission-critical diabetes management app
- 🔭 After hours: **[l8bin](https://github.com/mtsandeep/l8bin)**, a self-hosted app manager for side projects, and **[enoughwork](https://github.com/mtsandeep/enoughwork)**, which gently tells you to stop working
- 🧭 How I build: the right tool for the job, evolutionary architecture, observability from day one
